## 🌟 1. Problem Statement & Mission

India is home to over **7 million traditional artisans and weavers**, preserving centuries of cultural heritage — from *Jaipur Blue Pottery* and *Madhubani Art* to *Bastar Dhokra Casting*, *Kashmiri Pashmina*, and *Kutch Rogan & Embroidery*.

Despite unmatched craftsmanship, rural and marginalized artisans suffer from systemic market failures:
1. **Digital Literacy & Cataloging Gap:** Creating SEO-optimized product listings, high-converting descriptions, and structured metadata is intimidating without formal digital training.
2. **Pricing Disadvantage & Middleman Exploitation:** Artisans lack cost-transparency tools and often sell their work far below living wage rates to local middlemen.
3. **Language Barriers:** Inability to articulate craft heritage in English or corporate formats locks them out of modern buyers.
4. **Market Inaccessibility:** High-paying institutional buyers, boutique design studios, export houses, and corporate gifting curators struggle to discover authentic artisans directly.

### 💡 The CraftIQ Solution
**CraftIQ (कारिगरसेतु)** is **not just another generic e-commerce website**. It is an **AI-powered digital bridge and fair trade catalyst** that empowers low-digital-literacy artisans to generate professional bilingual catalogs with a single photo/voice prompt, calculate fair living wages, and connect directly with high-affinity buyers without commissions.

## 🚀 3. Key Feature Modules

### 📸 1. AI "Scan & Create" Smart Catalog Generator
- **Visual Texture & Pattern Recognition:** Analyzes craft imagery to detect craft type, materials, authentic technique, and estimated crafting hours.
- **Multilingual Cultural Storytelling:** Generates rich, authentic descriptions in both **English** and **Hindi (हिन्दी)** highlighting generational craft legacy, natural dyes, and care instructions.
- **Automated Metadata:** Auto-generates SEO tags, suggested price brackets, and buyer segment recommendations (B2B Boutique, Export, Eco-conscious Luxury).

### 🎙️ 2. Bilingual Voice-First Input (Hindi + English)
- Integrated Web Speech API with an offline fallback simulator.
- Rural artisans can simply speak in natural conversational Hindi to automatically fill catalog dimensions, materials, and crafting time.

### 💰 3. AI Fair Price & Dignified Wage Assistant
- Non-exploitative pricing calculator based on raw materials, crafting hours, and living wage rates.
- **Visual Cost Breakdown:** Interactive split bar displaying Raw Materials (35%), Fair Labor (45%), Overheads (10%), and Fair Margin (10%).
- Includes B2B bulk tier guidance and transparent disclaimers ensuring the artisan retains 100% price autonomy.

### 📱 4. Dual Artisan Mode (Digital Literacy Adaptability)
- **Simple Mode (Ultra-Accessible):** High-contrast cards, large touch targets, minimal text, and high-visibility action tiles (Add Product, Messages, Fair Price, Govt Schemes).
- **Pro Mode (Business Management):** Metric cards (Views, Direct Inquiries, 94% Fair Price Index, Estimated Earnings), AI Market Demand Insights, and real-time inventory management.

### 🛍️ 5. Heritage Discovery Marketplace (Buyer Experience)
- **Natural Language "Smart Search":** Query parser that understands queries like *"handmade pottery under 3000"* or *"madhubani painting blue"*.
- **"Why This Match?" Affinity Insights:** Clear explanations on why a product fits a buyer's interior style or corporate gifting theme.
- **Direct Maker Inquiry Engine:** Buyers send inquiries for custom orders or bulk purchases directly to the artisan's inbox with zero middleman cuts.

### 🏛️ 6. Government Schemes & Ecosystem Directory
- Direct links, eligibility criteria, and document checklists for official Indian government handicraft initiatives:
  - **PM Vishwakarma Scheme** (Credit support, toolkit incentive, skill training)
  - **ODOP (One District One Product)** (Export brand promotion)
  - **GeM Saras Collection** (Government department procurement)
  - **NHDP (National Handicrafts Development Programme)** (Textiles Ministry)

### 📊 7. Admin & National Impact Dashboard
- Active artisan clusters tracking across India (Rajasthan, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Kashmir, Gujarat).
- Category demand vs. supply forecasting charts powered by **Recharts**.
- Real-time impact trackers (Catalog Value, Direct Fair Trade Revenue, Income Uplift +42%).

## 🎯 5. Recommended 5-Minute Demo Flow for Evaluators

1. **Bilingual Switch:** On the top navbar, toggle between **English** and **हिन्दी** to view authentic bilingual localization across all screens.
2. **Simple Mode Experience:** Click **"Artisan View"** -> Click **"Switch to Simple Mode"** -> Explore the high-contrast interface designed for rural accessibility.
3. **AI Scan & Create:** Click **"नया उत्पाद जोड़ें" (Add Product)** -> Select any craft preset -> Watch the AI generate cultural stories, material tags, and price in real time.
4. **Fair Price Calculator:** Open **"Fair Price Calculator"** -> Adjust crafting hours and material costs -> Observe the living wage formula and cost breakdown.
5. **Buyer Search & Direct Inquiry:** Switch to **"Buyer Marketplace"** -> Search `pottery under 2000` -> Open product -> Click **"Send Direct Inquiry"**.
6. **Artisan Inbox:** Switch back to **"Artisan View"** -> Click **"Inquiries (पूछताछ)"** to view the inquiry received with zero middleman fees.
7. **National Analytics & Schemes:** Switch to **"Admin / Ecosystem"** -> View live cluster demand charts and government support initiatives.
