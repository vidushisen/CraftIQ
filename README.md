## 🌟 What is CraftIQ?
**CraftIQ** empowers rural & local artisans by using smart voice technology and AI image analysis to evaluate handicrafts, auto-generate tags, and connect directly with global buyers.
<div align="center">
  <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/xT9IgzoKnwFNmISR8I/giphy.gif" width="450px" alt="Artisan Craft Animation"/>

### ⚡ Highlights & Features
- 🎙️ **Voice AI Companion**: Speak in regional languages to list products.
- 🧠 **Smart Valuation**: AI-driven market pricing recommendations.
- 📱 **Offline-Ready PWA**: Works on low-end mobile devices without high-speed internet.
